Vicky & Xingyu

Trailer / Full Film

Vicky (Xingyu) Gu explains the complexities of being immersed in a new culture and learning to communicate in another language. Directed and animated by 15 students in USC's CTAN 470 Documentary Animation Production class, Spring 2020.
