Environment & Conservation

Mariam brings over fifteen years of storytelling, production, and program design experience to environmental and marine conservation work along the Red Sea. She is currently studying coral reef management, building on a personal connection to the sea that long predates the coursework.

Her focus is on the overlap between craft and cause: workshops that teach through story, films that make marine life legible to broad audiences, and content strategy for organizations working on conservation and environmental education.
